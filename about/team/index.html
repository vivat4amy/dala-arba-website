About

The Team

Named guides, regional partners, and the small office in Almaty that ties it together.

01 — Co-founder

Dauren Raptayev

Co-founder of Dala Arba Expeditions · Expedition leader, route designer, wilderness photographer

Dauren is a Kazakh explorer, expedition leader, wilderness photographer and route designer driven by a lifelong passion for remote landscapes and nomadic culture.

After leaving a successful career in investment banking in 2011, he began years of overland exploration across Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Tajikistan, Mongolia and the wider Central Asian region — through deserts, canyons, steppe, mountain passes and forgotten nomadic routes.

From 2015 to 2020, Dauren led major expedition projects across Kazakhstan with the Nomad Explorer movement, helping thousands of travellers discover the country's wild and culturally significant regions. In 2020 he founded YurtaDa — a network of authentic wilderness camps designed to reconnect travellers with nature and nomadic heritage. In 2023 he launched Dala Arba Expeditions.

Today, Dauren continues to design and lead expeditions across Central Asia, Mongolia and the Middle East — combining adventure, cultural discovery and remote off-road travel into experiences shaped by the spirit of the Great Steppe.

02 — Co-founder

Bauyrzhan Nigmetullin

Co-founder of Dala Arba Expeditions · Retired Air Force Colonel, security & expedition strategist

Bauyrzhan is a retired Air Force Colonel, international security expert and expedition strategist whose career combines leadership, diplomacy and operations in some of the world's most demanding environments.

With more than 30 years of experience in military aviation, peacekeeping operations and international cooperation, he served in senior positions within the Ministry of Defence of Kazakhstan — including Military Attaché to the United States and Head of the Kazakhstan Peacekeeping Operations Centre.

Throughout his career, Bauyrzhan worked closely with international organisations, foreign governments and global security institutions — leading strategic programmes, operational planning and multinational initiatives across Europe, the United States and Asia.

At Dala Arba Expeditions he brings expedition logistics, leadership under extreme conditions, operational discipline and deep international experience — helping shape safe, high-level journeys across the remote landscapes of Central Asia.

“True expedition travel requires not only adventure, but precision, responsibility and respect for the land and its people.”

03 — Senior researcher & expedition partner

Nick Fielding

British researcher, Fellow of the Royal Geographical Society, and author of Travellers in the Great Steppe and South to the Great Steppe

Nick is a British researcher, explorer, author and Fellow of the Royal Geographical Society. For over forty years he has travelled extensively across remote regions of Central Asia and Siberia, often on horseback.

A specialist in the history, culture and nomadic traditions of the Great Steppe, Nick is the author of several acclaimed books on Kazakhstan, Central Asia and Silk Road history — including Travellers in the Great Steppe and South to the Great Steppe. He is also editor of the English edition of Chokan Valikhanov's selected works.

Former senior reporter for The Sunday Times and investigative journalist for the Mail on Sunday, he is currently working on a new book about the Syr Darya River and the Northern Silk Road.

04 — Cultural heritage specialist

Elina Altynbekova

Cultural heritage specialist & expedition guide · archaeology, Silk Road history, nomadic culture

Elina is a cultural heritage specialist and expedition guide focused on the archaeology, nomadic traditions, and historical landscapes of Central Asia.

Working closely with the Ostrov Krym Scientific Restoration Laboratory, she contributes to cultural field programs, heritage interpretation, and historical storytelling connected to the civilizations of the Great Steppe.

Her work combines archaeological context, Silk Road history, nomadic culture, and immersive expedition experiences across Kazakhstan.

Through her involvement in cultural expeditions, museum collaborations, and heritage projects, she helps connect travellers with the deeper historical identity of the region.

05 — Cultural heritage partner

Dana Erik (Dana Bektaeva)

Researcher of Kazakh carpet heritage · Founder of the Kazakh Carpet Museum, Shymkent · Languages: Kazakh, Russian, English

Dana Erik is a Kazakh researcher, collector, and cultural advocate dedicated to preserving and interpreting the heritage of traditional Kazakh carpets. For more than a decade, she has documented, collected, and studied historic carpets, ornaments, and textile traditions, viewing them as a visual archive of nomadic knowledge, symbolism, and cultural memory.

She is the founder of the Kazakh Carpet Museum in Shymkent, the first private museum of its kind in Kazakhstan. Her collection includes more than 1,000 historic carpets, alongside traditional chests, yurt elements, musical instruments, and other ethnographic artifacts.

Dana's work focuses on decoding the symbolic language of Kazakh ornamentation and promoting the revival of traditional carpet weaving as an important element of Kazakhstan's cultural identity. Through exhibitions, lectures, research, and public engagement, she continues to advocate for the preservation of one of the oldest artistic traditions of the Great Steppe.
