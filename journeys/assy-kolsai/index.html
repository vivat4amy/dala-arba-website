Route Overview

A fully supported trek from Assy 2,600 m to Kolsai 1,818 m.

From Assy Plateau (2,600 m) over Kyzylauuz Pass (3,010 m) — the highest point — through the hidden Zhinizhke Valley, over Beskaragai Pass (2,400 m), down to the Chilyk River, finishing at Lower Kolsai Lake (1,818 m). Participants carry day packs only — all gear and meals transported by 4×4 support vehicles. 90 km over 5 trekking days.

Key Highlights

What you carry away

  • Kyzylauuz Pass (3,010 m) — panorama of Kungey Alatau glaciers
  • Zhinizhke Valley — lush hidden oasis, authentic kumiss and ayran from local families

 

 

  • Lower Kolsai Lake (1,818 m) — iconic turquoise mountain lake as the finish line
  • Fully supported format — day packs only, 4×4 vehicles carry all gear

Itinerary

Day by day

Day 00Almaty → Assy

140 km up to the plateau — the trek begins tomorrow

Transfer via Turgen Gorge to base camp at ~2,600 m, briefing and gear check before the first morning.

  • Afternoon — Almaty → Assy Plateau (140 km, 3.5 hrs via Turgen Gorge).
  • Evening — Camp setup at base (~2,600 m). Briefing & gear check.
Day 01Into Kyzylauuz

Alpine meadows, edelweiss, the first ford

16 km · 6–7 hrs. Sleep at 2,540 m.

  • Morning — Assy Plateau → Kyzylauuz Gorge. Alpine meadows.
  • Midday — Assy River ford (knee-deep, July). Edelweiss fields.
  • Evening — Camp at 2,540 m.
Day 02The pass

KEY DAY — Kyzylauuz Pass (3,010 m) into Zhinizhke

18–20 km · 7–8 hrs. Sleep at 1,810 m. The most demanding day of the trek.

  • Morning — Ascent to Kyzylauuz Pass (3,010 m). Trekking poles essential.
  • Midday — Summit: Kungey Alatau panorama, glaciers.
  • Afternoon — Long scree descent into the Zhinizhke Valley. Camp at 1,810 m.
Day 03Hidden valley

Through juniper forests to a working aul

18–20 km · 7–8 hrs. Sleep at 1,990 m.

  • Morning — Zhinizhke Valley → Interfluve Plateau. Juniper forests.
  • Afternoon — Zhinizhke village — fresh kumiss & ayran from local families.
  • Evening — Camp at 1,990 m.
Day 04To the Chilyk

Beskaragai Pass and the long view back

14 km · 5–6 hrs. Sleep at 1,500 m.

  • Morning — Beskaragai Pass (2,400 m). Sweeping Kungey Alatau views.
  • Afternoon — Descent to the Chilyk River.
  • Evening — Campfire and fishing. Camp at 1,500 m.
Day 05Finish at Kolsai

Out of the mountains, into the turquoise

15–16 km. The trek ends at Lower Kolsai Lake (1,818 m); transfer to Almaty on arrival.

  • Morning — Chilyk River → Lower Kolsai Lake (1,818 m).
  • Afternoon — Transfer to Almaty.

Before you book

How the trek runs

Five demanding days of mountain trekking — fully supported, but real altitude and real distance. You carry a day pack; the camp travels with you.

◌

Activity level

Challenging ★★★★. 5 trekking days, 14–20 km each, with one 3,010 m pass. Prior multi-day mountain experience required. Strong knees for long scree descents.

◇

Camp & meals

5 tented camp nights at pre-scouted locations. Hot breakfast + trail lunch pack + hot dinner daily. Tents, bio-toilet and solar shower provided.

⌖

Access & transfers

Transfers Almaty → Assy and Kolsai → Almaty included. 2–3 guides & cooks travel with the group. 4×4 support vehicles carry all expedition gear.

☼

Season

July–August only. Outside these months passes are snowbound or river fords impassable. Departures confirmed by weather and group readiness.
